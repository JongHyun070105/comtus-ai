## Rule admission policy

An external formula can enter `game_rules` only after recording the exact page/post, author/source class, publication or check date, applicable game version/mode, terms/license note, assumptions, and a reproducible comparison. Until then, skill scores remain advisory and training/refresh formulas remain unverified. No source here establishes an 80% win rate or a ranking-challenge win-probability model.
